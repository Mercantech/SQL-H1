Use a realistic elephant head structure: a long naturally tapered trunk with gentle low curl, broad angular elephant ear folds and natural forehead contours. Make the eyes about half as large relative to the head as in the source, with small natural brown irises, modest human-scale visible whites, no oversized sparkle highlights. Use a small restrained friendly closed-mouth smile, thoughtful warm attentive expression, approachable professor rather than stern. Remove all fluffy or spiky hairstyle; the head has natural elephant skin and no hairdo. Slightly longer adult torso and legs, normally proportioned hands and feet instead of oversized cartoon paws. Sophisticated contemporary editorial illustration for a university textbook or cultured magazine, controlled soft gouache color planes with subtle precise ink details, clean minimal surface treatment with much less fuzzy texture. Muted slate blue skin and warm greige, oatmeal and brown palette. Refined quiet educated visual personality for adult technical students. Plain warm ivory backdrop, no scenery, full character including feet and pointer with clear generous margins, square composition. Keep character original; no PostgreSQL logo or other logo, no text or lettering, no watermark, no collage, no labels.
